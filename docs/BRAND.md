# WhisperGuard Brand Brief

## Tagline

Speak up. Stay in control.

## Key Messages

- Designed for workers as individual users, not as an employer-controlled HR intake form.
- A future privacy model should disclose only a commitment and a verifiable eligibility result, never an incident narrative on a public ledger.
- The current prototype has no active anonymity or report-delivery protections; user safety comes before promotional claims.

## Palette

- Forest: `#17483B`
- Soft paper: `#F2F5EF`
- Mint: `#DCEFE4`
- Lime accent: `#C9ED7A`
- Warm alert: `#A64D3C`

## X Profile Bio

Privacy-first workplace reporting concept. Built around worker control. Prototype only; no real reports are collected.

## X Banner Concept

Use an unframed forest-green wordmark and a quiet paper-texture field, with the tagline at left and a small “concept prototype” label. Avoid locks or shields that imply proven security.
